Questions buyers and sales teams actually ask

Real pre-deal verification questions, answered only with capabilities FALCORA actually has.

How do you verify an overseas buyer before wiring a deposit?

Check at least three things: whether the company is real and still operating, whether its website and corporate contacts line up, and whether it appears independently in trade show exhibitor records, association member directories or public tender notices. FALCORA lists each item with a clickable public source and a retrieval date.

How can you tell if a foreign company is a shell company?

We do not make that call for you, and we do not produce a risk score. We output three states — found, found but empty, not found. A missing value is labelled as missing, never back-filled. The judgement stays with you.

How do you verify a business contact email or phone number?

Only publicly listed role-based corporate contacts (L2), each with its own source link and retrieval date. Personal contacts (L3) are user-supplied only; we never collect, infer or display them.

What should a pre-deal due diligence checklist include?

As measured from our own data files (2026-11): 75,180 trade show exhibitor records, 3,775 association member records and 1,763 public tender notices across 100+ country and territory codes. The three directories never stand in for one another, and suppliers are never mixed with buyers.
