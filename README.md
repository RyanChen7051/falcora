# FALCORA

**Global B2B intelligence & pre-deal due diligence — know who you're dealing with, worldwide.**

FALCORA helps sales and BD teams confirm, *before* signing or wiring a deposit, that the company on the other side is real, still operating, and worth their time. Every conclusion carries a clickable public source and a retrieval date.

## What it does

1. **Find buyers worldwide** — by industry, region and purchasing intent.
2. **Verify before you talk** — cross-check trade-show exhibitor records, association membership and public tender notices to confirm a company is really operating.
3. **Keep auditable evidence** — export an intel pack where every conclusion links back to a public source.

## Data boundaries

- **Three-state output**: found / found but empty / not found. The platform **never outputs "no risk"**.
- **L1** public company information · **L2** corporate contacts (source + retrieval date on every line) · **L3** personal contacts, user-supplied only.
- Personal contacts are **never collected, inferred or shown**.
- Directory cross-checks prove a company is operating; they are **not** a buying-intent or credit-rating conclusion.
