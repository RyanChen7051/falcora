How to use this checklist

Work top to bottom and fill in one row per check. The last column is the one people skip: it tells you exactly what to write when a source comes back with nothing. A checklist that only has room for positive results will quietly push you toward inventing them.

FoundValue present, with source URL and retrieval date.
Found but emptySource reached, no value for this field.
Not foundNo result returned. Do not substitute.

There is no no-risk state and no score. A completed checklist is an evidence file, not a verdict; the decision stays with the person authorising the payment.

HTTP 200 is not a verdict

A 200 response only means something answered. A 403 or 429 usually means anti-bot protection, not a closed company. A local filtering page can impersonate a response. Record the state you observed rather than converting it into a claim about whether the business is alive.

Common questions

What should a pre-deal due diligence checklist include?

Four groups: entity existence, digital footprint, independent operating evidence, and role-based corporate contacts plus the evidence record itself. Each check needs a public source, the exact value found, a retrieval date, and one of three states: found, found but empty, or not found.

How many sources are enough to verify an overseas buyer?

There is no magic number, but no single source should carry the conclusion. Aim for two or three unrelated public sources that name the same entity, and keep them separate in the file. One directory entry is a lead, not verification.

What does found but empty mean?

The source was reached successfully but contains no value for that field. It is different from not found, where no result was returned at all. Both are recorded as-is. Neither is back-filled from another directory, and neither is silently turned into a plausible value.

Can I use a trade show exhibitor list as buyer evidence?

No. Exhibitor and association membership directories are supplier-side records. A company appearing there shows it exhibited or held a membership, not that it buys from you. Public tender notices are buyer-side evidence. Tag every record with its side and never merge the two.
